Hello and Namaste! I am Priyanshu R Gupta, a PhD student in environmental Engineering at UT Austin.
Prior to UT, I completed my undergaduate (2015-19) and my masters (2020-22) in Chemical Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar and at IIT Bombay respectively.
